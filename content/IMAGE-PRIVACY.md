## Visual clue quality

Every playable location image was reviewed against this minimum bar in August 2026:

- The image visibly shows food stalls, a communal dining area, or an unmistakable market/food-centre entrance.
- Generic residential blocks, service alleys, retail-only stalls and distant roofs do not qualify.
- The scene must belong to the exact centre named by the question and retain a verifiable source page and reusable licence.

Seven weak images were replaced during this review. Adam Road Food Centre retained its existing view because the only other verified Commons image was a darker, less informative exterior.
