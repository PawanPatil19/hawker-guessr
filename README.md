How well do you really know Singapore's hawker centres?

Hawker Guessr is a daily game that gives you five photos of hawker centres from around Singapore. Look closely for familiar signs, buildings and neighbourhood clues, then place your guess on the map.

The closer your pin is to the real location, the more points you score.

## How to play

1. Look at the hawker centre photo.
2. Drop a pin where you think it was taken.
3. Find out how close you were and learn something about the location.
4. Play all five rounds and share your result with friends.

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, so there is always someone to compare scores with. Your shared result does not reveal the answers, and you do not need an account to play.

Whether you know every corner of Singapore or just want to discover a few new makan spots, give today's puzzle a try.
