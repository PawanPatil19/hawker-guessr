Hawker
Guessr 🇸🇬

One photo. One pin. Five rounds. Every morning at 6am.
The look is not "food app" — it is hawker centre signage: enamel plates, stall-number tiles, terrazzo, dot-matrix price boards.

Palette

Flat, printed, high contrast. Dark mode is the same structure at night-market temperature.

Type: Oswald (condensed, uppercase) for display — reads as signboard. Inter for body, must sit beside Chinese stall names without looking broken. IBM Plex Mono tabular figures for every number: scores, distances, prices, countdowns.

Motif rules

ElementRule
Corners0px radius everywhere. Enamel plates don't have rounded corners.
Borders2px solid ink. No shadows, no blur, no glass.
Round indicatorFive stall-number tiles 01–05. Fill chilli as completed, kaya for current. Signature element — appears on game, card, and share image.
NumbersTabular figures, always. A jittering countdown looks broken.
Reveal animation~1.8s: photo lifts, line draws pin→truth, distance counts up, fact card slides. Skippable on tap. Polish this above all else.
Touch targetsMin 48px. Uncles are the audience with the best answers and the worst eyesight.
Photo creditAlways visible when a photo isn't yours. Non-negotiable.
